Receipts

Install this app first. In Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”, and open it from there. Receipts saved in Safari don’t carry over to the installed app.

Settings

Before your first receipt

  1. On the Concur website, open Profile Settings and verify the email address you’ll send from. Concur rejects other senders.
  2. Send from that same account in your email app.
  3. After sending, look for the receipt under Available Receipts in Concur.

Tap Share and save to OneDrive. Tap Open Outlook, attach the file from OneDrive, and send it to . Then tap Mark sent.
